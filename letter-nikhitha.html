Dear Anu ❤️,

You became my friend during the first year of B.Tech, and honestly, I never imagined that our friendship would become this special and create so many memories. 🥹❤️

One of the funniest things about our friendship is that at least once every week, we somehow end up fighting or arguing. 😂 We get angry, we fight, we irritate each other, and then somehow we come back together as if nothing ever happened. 😂❤️ I guess that is just our way of being friends.

Our ways of thinking are completely different. 😂 For example, I am someone who thinks about everything beforehand and worries about what might happen, while you are the type who can go through a situation that looks like life or death and somehow come back alive like nothing happened! 😭😂

Whenever you are around, there is never a boring moment. You bring so much comedy and entertainment into my life. 😂 Maha and Anu together are more chaotic than Tom and Jerry! 🐱🐭😂 When Maha and Anu are together, even Tom and Jerry would feel like they are watching professionals! 😂🐱🐭 Somehow, every simple conversation turns into a full comedy episode.

And yes, you are very beautiful. ❤️✨ But more than your beauty, I love your personality, your energy, your craziness, and the way you can make even ordinary moments memorable. 🫶🏻

You are the second person I met in my B.Tech journey, but somehow you have given me even more memories than the first person I met. 🥹❤️ From silly fights and random conversations to laughing over absolutely stupid things, there are so many moments with you that I will always remember.

And of course… my personal makeup artist! 💄😂❤️ I don't know what I would do without you whenever I need my makeup done. 😂 One day when you become a famous makeup artist, just remember that I was one of your earliest clients! 😌💄😂

Jokes apart, I am genuinely very happy and grateful to have a friend like you in my life. ❤️ Some friendships are peaceful, some are serious, but ours is a beautiful mixture of fights, laughter, craziness, memories, and love. 🫂✨

One thing I really want to tell you is — please always take care of yourself and be careful. ❤️ You are brave and adventurous, but I want you to always come back safely from all your adventures. 😂😭 Your safety will always matter to the people who love you.

On your birthday, I truly wish that you shine in everything you do. ✨🌸 I hope you achieve all your dreams, succeed in every field you choose, and become the person you always want to be. May you always have reasons to smile, people who genuinely care about you, and the courage to face every challenge that comes your way. 💪🏻❤️

I hope that even after B.Tech, when life takes us in different directions, we will still remember all these crazy moments and laugh about them together. 🥹❤️ One day, we should look back at our old photos and say, “How were we even like this?” 😂

Thank you for being my friend, my fighting partner, my comedy partner, my personal makeup artist 😂💄, and most importantly, one of the most memorable people in my B.Tech life. ❤️

Maha & Anu — unlimited comedy 😂❤️

Happy Birthday once again, Anu! 🎂🥳💗

Stay beautiful. ✨

No matter how many times we fight, I hope we always find our way back to being the same crazy friends. 🫂❤️
