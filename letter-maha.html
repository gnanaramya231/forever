A heartfelt birthday letter from Mahalakshmi... ❤️

Dear Anu ❤️,

First of all, Many Many More Happy Returns of the Day! 🎂🥳❤️ Nee life lo ee birthday chala happiness, peace, success and beautiful memories tho nindipovali ani heartful ga wish chesthunna. ✨🫂

Mana bond gurinchi alochisthe, nijam ga naake konchem surprising ga anipisthundi. 😅❤️

First year lo ninnu chudagane naaku oka akka laanti feeling vachindi. ❤️ Appude mana madhya oka genuine bond untundi ani anukunna. 🥹❤️ But second year lo manam antha close ga undevallam kaadu. Third year lo manaki konni misunderstandings kuda ayyayi. 🥺

Kaani life lo malli kalisam… and final year ki manam intha close ela ayyamoo naake ippatiki artham kaadu. 😭❤️ Literally, ippatiki kuda nenu nammalekapothunna.

Naa kosam beauty parlour lo almost four hours antha patience tho spend chesav 😭😂. Nenu ekkadiki veldham anna, nuvvu “yes” ane cheptav, “no” ane cheppavu 😂❤️. Shopping ki aina, ekkadikaina vellali anna… naatho vachestav. Beauty-related things cheppadam, avi discuss cheyyadam… Ivi bayata chusthe chala small things laga anipinchachu, kaani naaku matram avi chala special memories. 🫂❤️

I think mana bond intha close avvadaniki oka important reason, nuvvu personal issues lo unnappudu, nuvvu cry chesinappudu ninnu ala chusthe naaku kuda chala badha anipinchedi. 🥺

“Raa… mundu tinadaniki” ani entha adigano naake telusu. 😭 Nuvvu first tinaledu… kaani naa maata ki respect ichi, finally vachi naaku tinipinchavu. ❤️

Naa maata ki nuvvu ichina aa respect naaku chala special ga anipinchindi. 🫂❤️ Aa moment ni nenu eppatiki marchiponu.

Kaani recent ga oka chinna issue valla… nenu “idi adagoddu” ani cheppina maata oka person ki cheppav. Aa time lo nijam ga nee meeda naaku unna impression motham change ayipoyindi. 🥺

Nijam cheppalante, appati varaku nenu ninnu oka sister la, oka very good friend la chusukunna. Nuvvu eppudaina problem lo unte neeku help avvali, neeku useful ga undali, na valla possible ayina oka chinna help aina cheyali ani genuinely anukunna. ❤️

Kaani aa incident valla ninnu completely trust cheyyali ane feelings konchem disturb ayyindi. Ninnu bad person ani anukovatledu… but aa situation naaku chala hurt ayindi. 🥺

Still, mana friendship naaku important kabatte ee maata honest ga chepthunna. ❤️‍🩹 Mana old closeness malli slow ga return avuthundi ani genuinely hope chesthunna. 🫂 Malli previous la comfortable ga, open ga, oka sister-sister bond la undagaligite naaku chala happy ga untundi. ❤️

Mana madhya enni misunderstandings vachina, enni ups and downs vachina… mana journey lo unna beautiful memories matram naaku eppatiki special. 🥹✨

Manam iddaram ❤️

Once again, Happiest Birthday Anu! 🎂🥳❤️ Nee life lo nuvvu korukunna prati okkati jaragali. ✨ Always happy ga undali, always smile chesthu undali. 😍🫂 And mana bond kuda ilage beautiful ga continue avvali. ❤️‍🩹✨

Once again, Happy Birthday Anu! 😍🎂🫂❤️

With lots of love,

Your Mahalakshmi ❤️✨
